COLLEGE AWARENESS

About your signup.

When you enter your email to unlock this guide, College Awareness saves that email in its Systeme.io contact list with the “Download” tag. If your email is already in the list, the tag is added to the existing contact.

Why we collect your email

Your email records your request for the resource. The signup form does not ask for your name, school, financial details, or payment information.

How access works

After your signup and tag are confirmed, this site sets an essential access cookie in your browser. It lasts for 24 hours and lets you reopen or download the guide on this device. The cookie contains no email address. Clearing it or letting it expire requires another signup to unlock the guide.

Services used

Systeme.io stores the contact information, and Vercel hosts the website and processes the signup request. These services may process technical data needed to operate the site. You can read their Systeme.io privacy policy and Vercel privacy notice.

This page explains the data collected by this signup form. If you receive marketing emails, use the unsubscribe link in those messages to stop receiving them.
